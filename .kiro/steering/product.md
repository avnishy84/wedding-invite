# Product
A single-page Indian wedding invitation website. Vintage ethnic aesthetic.
Primary users: guests on mobile phones via WhatsApp link, ages 15 to 75.

Principles:
- Warm, respectful, celebratory tone. Never flashy or startup-like.
- Mobile-first. Large readable text. Tap targets of at least 44px.
- Every section earns its place: Hero, Blessing, Couple, Events, Venue, RSVP, Gallery, Footer.
- Bilingual (EN default, HI toggle). All user-facing strings live in the content file.
- No tracking beyond privacy-friendly basic analytics (optional). No third-party ads or cookies.
Out of scope: auth, payments, CMS, admin panel, SSR.
